Classic game icon images are derived from Noto Color Emoji artwork by Google LLC and contributors.
Upstream: https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji
Source font version: 2.051 (e92753bfa55fd449e427d4d325f9c8c40408c74e).
Bitmap glyph images were extracted, resampled, and encoded as WebP for the game.
The shield, skull, tools and parcel images received a minor contrast adjustment.
